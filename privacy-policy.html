Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

NutriMealSnap (“we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by Matta Santoshi, provides a mobile app for meal logging and general nutrition tracking. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it is shared, and how you can make privacy requests.

For privacy questions, contact nutrimealsnap@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information: your email address, name, account identifier, and sign-in provider details.
  • Profile information: details you enter, such as age, sex, height, weight, activity level, country or region, calorie goals, and nutrition targets.
  • Nutrition and meal information: meal names, food and portion estimates, calories, nutrients, meal dates, water logs, and other information you choose to record.
  • Meal photos and descriptions: photos or text descriptions you submit for meal analysis. Submitted photos or descriptions are sent to the meal-analysis service to generate estimates. A compressed photo may be stored privately with your account after you confirm a scan.
  • Voice input: when you use voice entry, your device’s speech-recognition service converts speech into text. The app uses the resulting text for meal analysis. Audio handling depends on the speech-recognition service and your device settings.
  • Barcode information: the barcode number and lookup request may be sent to the configured food-information service when you use barcode lookup.
  • App and device information: basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app, such as app version, device type, and error details, where collected.

Please avoid including unrelated personal or sensitive information in meal photos, descriptions, or support messages.

How we use information

We use information to create and maintain your account; analyze meals and provide food, portion, and nutrition estimates; save and display your meals, goals, and nutrition history; provide features such as barcode lookup, voice entry, reminders, and subscriptions when available; respond to support requests; secure and troubleshoot the service; and meet legal requirements.

Nutrition estimates are intended for general tracking. They may be inaccurate or incomplete and are not medical advice.

When information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide and protect the service, including with:

  • Supabase, for account authentication, database, and storage services.
  • Google Gemini, to analyze meal photos or descriptions submitted for meal analysis.
  • Your device’s speech-recognition provider, when you use voice input.
  • The configured food-information provider, when you use barcode lookup.
  • Google Play, as applicable, to process purchases and provide subscription status.
  • Service providers that help us operate, secure, or support NutriMealSnap, where configured.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect users, our rights, or the service.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their handling of information is also governed by their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Storage, security, and retention

We use service-provider security controls intended to protect information in transit and in storage. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We keep account and nutrition information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Some information may remain for a limited time in backups or be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can review and update profile information in the app where those controls are available. You may request deletion of your account and associated data:

  • In the app: Profile → Request account deletion.
  • On the website: use the NutriMealSnap account deletion request page.
  • By email: request account deletion from NutriMealSnap Support.

We may ask you to verify your account before completing a request. We will handle requests as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

To request deletion on the web: visit the account deletion page. Do not send your password.

Children’s privacy

NutriMealSnap is a general nutrition-tracking app and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, our data practices, or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide additional notice where required.
