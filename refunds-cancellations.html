Refunds & cancellations

Last updated: 8 October 2026

NutriMealSnap subscriptions are purchased through Google Play. This page explains how cancellation and refund requests are handled for those purchases.

Cancel a subscription

You can cancel anytime in your Google Play subscription settings. Cancellation stops future renewals. Access usually continues until the end of the current billing period.

Open Google Play subscription settings ↗

Request a refund

Refund requests are handled under Google Play’s refund rules and applicable local laws. Eligibility and available options may depend on the purchase, the time since purchase, and your location.

Read Google Play’s refund guidance ↗

Need help?

For help with a NutriMealSnap subscription, contact NutriMealSnap@gmail.com. Include the email address associated with your NutriMealSnap account and relevant order information. Never send your password or full payment-card details.
