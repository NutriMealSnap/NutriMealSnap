Request account deletion

NutriMealSnap account and associated data

You can request deletion of your NutriMealSnap account and associated data from this page, even if you no longer have the app installed.

How to submit a request

  1. Email NutriMealSnap Support from the email address linked to your account. You do not need to reinstall or open the app.
  2. Use the subject NutriMealSnap account deletion request.
  3. Do not include your password or payment-card details.

Email a deletion request ↗

What happens next

We may ask you to verify that you own the account before processing the request. We will delete the account and associated personal data, except information we must retain where required or permitted by law, security, or fraud-prevention needs. Any retained information will be handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting your NutriMealSnap account does not automatically cancel a subscription purchased through Google Play. Cancel it separately in your Google Play subscription settings.
